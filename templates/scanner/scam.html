Check a message

Paste an SMS or WhatsApp message you are not sure about. Do not click its links, call its numbers or reply. The checker only reads the text.

Up to 3,000 characters. The message is checked and then forgotten. Only a short label (the first 50 characters, with long numbers hidden) and the result are saved.

Try an example

What the checker looks for

  • Requests for an OTP, PIN or password
  • Requests for money, fees, deposits or QR payments
  • Threats about your account, card, SIM or KYC
  • Prize, lottery and easy-job offers
  • Police, tax or government officers contacting you
  • Fake delivery messages and app installs
  • A known company name with a link to another website
  • Dangerous or shortened links (checked by the URL scanner)

This is a rule-based check, not AI. It knows English and some common Hinglish phrases. It can miss new tricks, and it can flag honest messages that use scary words.
