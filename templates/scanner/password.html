Check a password

Type a password to see how strong it is. It is checked by this app only. It is never saved, never written to a log and never sent to anyone, not even to the AI.

Up to 128 characters. For extra safety, test a password that is similar to yours (same length and kinds of characters) instead of the real one.

Try an example

What the checker looks for

  • Length, which matters most
  • Lowercase and capital letters, numbers and symbols
  • Very common passwords, also with numbers or look-alike letters added
  • Sequences like abc or 123, and keyboard patterns like qwer
  • Repeated characters and repeated pieces
  • Your own username or email inside the password

This is a rule-based check, not AI, and it does not look at lists of leaked passwords. A good score is not a promise. Use a different password for every account.

How your password is protected

  • It is never saved. Only the result is kept, such as "Weak, 35 of 100".
  • It is never written to a log and never put in an address.
  • It is never sent to the AI or to any other service.
  • If your browser offers to save it, choose "Never".
