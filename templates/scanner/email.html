Try an example

What the checker looks for

  • Requests for passwords, PINs, OTPs or card details
  • Requests for money, fees or gift cards
  • Urgent wording, threats and "verify your account"
  • Prize, lottery and refund offers
  • A sender that pretends to be a known company
  • A Reply-To address that differs from the sender
  • Dangerous links (each link is checked by the URL scanner)
  • Risky attachment names and "enable macros"

This is a rule-based check, not AI. It can miss new tricks, and it can flag honest emails that use scary words.
