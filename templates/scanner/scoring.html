How scoring works

Every scanner follows the same rules. Each warning sign adds points, and the total decides the level. Nothing is random, and every rule is listed here.

From points to a level

  1. The scanner looks for warning signs in the text. It never opens links, files or apps.
  2. Each warning sign adds the points shown in the tables below.
  3. The total is the risk score. It is limited to 100.
  4. The score decides the level. A score of {{ threat_min_score }} or more (Medium and above) counts as a threat and raises an alert.
